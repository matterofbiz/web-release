04. Privacy First

Local-First Storage

spheRate has no server that receives your feedback. Your vault lives on your device, and you back it up by exporting a file.

Local Storage

Your Data Stays On Your Device

spheRate keeps your vault in your device's own app storage and makes no network connections. The spheRate developers keep no copy of your records. spheRate does not add its own encryption, so your data is protected by your device lock. Export a backup file to keep it safe.

Integrity Checksums

Integrity Checksum on Every Entry

Each feedback entry stores a SHA-256 checksum of its content, so accidental corruption can be spotted. Rater names and emails are self-reported and are not verified, and the checksum is not a signature.
