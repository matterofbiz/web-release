Data Sovereignty & Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 · Version 1.3
The short version

spheRate is a local-first personal feedback app. Your reviews, ratings and goals are stored on your device, and the app makes no network connections: no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The spheRate developers never receive your data. You can export a backup file and import it again whenever you like.

01. Scope and Fundamental Principles

This Privacy Policy covers the spheRate Android app, the spheRate web app, and this website. spheRate has no user accounts and no spheRate-run database of user feedback.

  • No trackers: The app and this website do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels or tracking cookies.
  • No central copy: Your feedback is not sent to the spheRate developers, and is not shared with your employer, school or other organizations unless you export or send it yourself.
  • No account: You do not sign in to anything to use spheRate.
  • Data minimization: spheRate processes only what it needs to compute your Personal Net Promoter Score and show your dashboard.

02. Where Your Data Is Stored

spheRate stores your 360-degree feedback, ratings, sphere tags and goals in your device's app storage (IndexedDB and local storage of the app's web view, or of your browser on the web).

How Storage Works
  • On your device only: Data stays on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • No cloud copy: Because nothing is uploaded, nobody can restore your data for you. If you lose the phone, reset it, uninstall the app or clear its storage, your data is gone unless you exported a backup file first.
  • Backups are files you control: You can export everything to a file and import it later (see section 06).

03. Network Connections and Third Parties

spheRate makes no network connections while you use it. The app does not contact analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, font, avatar, AI, sign-in or cloud-storage services. Rater profile pictures are never loaded from the internet.

  • Files and invitations you send: When you export a backup, a PDF report or an .ics calendar file, or send a feedback invitation by email, the file or message goes to the app you pick (email, Drive, messaging, files and so on). The spheRate developers do not see it, and that app's provider's terms apply.
  • Reminders: Month-end reminders are scheduled on your device. No push-notification service is involved.
  • Links: If you tap a link, for example to open Google Play, it opens in another app or your browser, which is governed by that site's own policy.
  • This website: The overview and policy pages are static pages that load no third-party scripts, fonts or images. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested).

04. No AI Processing

spheRate does not send your feedback to any AI, large language model or machine-learning service, and does not use it to train models.

  • Computed on your device: Personal Net Promoter Score, sphere breakdowns, trait summaries and growth prompts are produced on your device by fixed, rule-based calculations.
  • If this changes: We will update this policy first, and no feedback will leave your device for such a purpose without your explicit opt-in.

05. People in Your Feedback

Feedback includes personal information about other people: the name and email a rater types, their comments, and their relationship to you. These details are stored exactly as entered, only on your device (and in any backup file you create). spheRate does not verify them and does not sign raters in through Google, LinkedIn or any other identity provider.

Because the spheRate developers hold no copy, a rater who wants their feedback corrected or removed needs to ask you, the person who holds it. You are responsible for how you use and share what others have told you, including any backup file you make.

Each entry also stores a SHA-256 checksum of its content so that accidental corruption can be noticed. The checksum is not a digital signature and does not prevent someone with access to your data from editing an entry. It is computed on your device.

06. Export, Import, Deletion and Your Choices

  • Export: In the app, open Data & Backup and choose Export backup file. This saves everything as one JSON file (not encrypted). You can also export a PDF report or .ics calendar reminders. On Android these open the system share sheet so you choose where the file goes.
  • Import: In Data & Backup, choose Choose backup file. The file is checked on your device and nothing changes until you confirm. You can add only what is missing, or replace everything on the device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Delete everything: In Data & Backup, choose Delete all data. This removes your feedback and goals from the device. Backup files you exported and copies you shared are not deleted by the app: delete those yourself.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently removes the data stored on the device.
  • Your rights: Depending on where you live you may have rights over personal data. Because we do not hold your feedback, the controls above are the fastest way to use them. You can also contact us below.

07. Security

  • spheRate does not add its own encryption to data stored on your device or to exported files. They are protected by your device lock.
  • Anyone who can unlock your device, or who gets a copy of your backup file, can read your spheRate data. Use a screen lock and keep backup files in a private place.
  • Because the app makes no network connections, your data is not sent over the internet by spheRate.

08. Changes to This Policy

We will change the effective date and version above whenever this policy changes. Version 1.3 (November 6, 2026) removes the optional Google Drive backup and Google sign-in described in earlier drafts: spheRate no longer connects to Google Drive or to any other service, and backups are export and import files that you control.
