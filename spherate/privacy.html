Data Sovereignty & Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 31, 2026 · Version 1.0
The spheRate Sovereignty Pledge

spheRate is built from the ground up as a zero-knowledge, client-side personal feedback vault. We believe your reputation, ratings, and peer feedback belong exclusively to you—not to corporate HR silos, advertising tracking networks, or central servers.

01. Scope and Fundamental Principles

This Privacy Policy governs the spheRate web application, mobile bindings, and associated services. By using spheRate, you benefit from a local-first software architecture designed to operate without central tracking, user profiling, or secondary data sales.

  • Zero Tracking Cookies: We do not deploy behavioral tracking cookies, Facebook Pixels, Google Analytics scripts, or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No HR Centralization: Your data is never shared with or exposed to your employer, school, or external organizations unless you explicitly export or present your record.
  • Data Minimization: We only process data required to compute your Net Promoter Score and render your local feedback dashboard.

02. Information Storage and Local Sovereignty

Unlike legacy cloud software, spheRate stores your primary application data—including 360-degree reviews, behavioral ratings, sphere tags, and goal progress—directly inside your client browser storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage).

How Storage Works
  • Local Browser Storage: Reviews and ratings remain on your physical device until cleared or overwritten by you.
  • Google Drive BYOS (Bring Your Own Storage): When you enable cloud backup, spheRate authenticates using OAuth 2.0 strictly requesting the isolated https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope.
  • Isolated App Sandbox: This scope restricts spheRate to a single hidden data file inside your personal Google Drive account. spheRate cannot read, scan, or access any other documents or files in your Google Drive.

03. No Artificial Intelligence (AI) Model Processing

spheRate does NOT use Artificial Intelligence (AI), Large Language Models (LLMs), or third-party Machine Learning services (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic) to process, analyze, or train on your feedback data.

  • 100% Deterministic Local Computing: All calculations—including Personal Net Promoter Score aggregation, 4-Sphere score distribution, behavioral trait breakdown, and growth prompts—are performed entirely on your client device using local, deterministic heuristic algorithms.
  • Zero Model Training: Your review entries, ratings, comments, and personal information are never transmitted to any AI provider or used for model training or machine intelligence datasets.
  • Complete Algorithmic Transparency: Growth prompts and feedback synthesis rely exclusively on transparent mathematical formulas and local rule-based heuristics.

04. Rater Details and Integrity Checksums

When someone submits feedback, the name and email they type are stored with their response exactly as entered. spheRate does not verify them and does not sign raters in through Google, LinkedIn or any other identity provider.

Each entry also stores a SHA-256 checksum of its content so that accidental corruption can be noticed. The checksum is not a digital signature and does not prevent someone with access to your data from editing an entry. It is computed on your device.

05. Data Export, Portability, and Deletion Rights

You retain 100% control over your data lifecycle at all times.

  • Instant JSON Export: Export your complete reputation vault, feedback history, and quarterly goals as a JSON file (not encrypted) with a single click.
  • Calendar Sync (RFC 5545): Export review reminders directly to iCal / Google Calendar format (.ics).
  • Right to Erase: You may instantly purge all local application data via the Settings & Storage view. Deleting the app data file from your Google Drive appDataFolder permanently removes all cloud backup copies.

06. Security Standards

Security measures:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all HTTPS network communication.
  • spheRate does not add its own encryption to data stored on your device, in your Google Drive app folder, or in exported JSON files. Those are protected by your device security and your Google account.
  • OAuth 2.0 token isolation with zero server-side credential persistence.
