Under the hood.

Enterprise-grade machine learning, quantized for mobile.

Whisper Small (Q8_0)

Sub-second acoustic transcription powered by highly optimized whisper.cpp integration. We utilize an 8-bit quantized Whisper model to achieve near-human transcription accuracy efficiently on Android hardware.

Qwen2.5 1.5B Instruct

Intelligent offline summarization driven by llama.cpp. This 1.5B parameter reasoning engine (~1.1 GB) is heavily prompted to generate structured, actionable executive outputs natively on your device.

Natural Audio Engineering

Unlike standard apps, EdgeDraft bypasses Automatic Gain Control (AGC) to preserve natural speech dynamics. Faint audio is actively monitored, gracefully generating [unclear] tags instead of leaving silent gaps.

Dynamic Delivery

To adhere to Play Store limits, the core application remains lightweight. Heavy neural weights are fetched to internal storage only upon first launch via a dedicated WorkManager background downloader.
