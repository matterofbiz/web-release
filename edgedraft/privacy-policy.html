EdgeDraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Version: 2026.1 Provided by: Business Research & Applications Limited ("we", "us") App: EdgeDraft for Android

1. The short version

  • EdgeDraft turns speech into text, summaries and action items on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your recordings, transcripts, summaries or contacts.
  • Audio from the microphone is processed in memory and thrown away as soon as it has been turned into text. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.
  • Your transcripts and summaries stay on your phone, encrypted. The app has no app lock, so anyone who can unlock your phone can open it.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app uses the internet to download its AI model files and, through Google Play, for installs, updates and purchases. Those services see your IP address (section 4).
  • Files you export, share or copy leave the app's protection. What happens to them is up to you.

4. What does leave your phone

AI model downloads. On first setup the app downloads two model files (a speech model and a summary model, over 1 GB in total). By default they come from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and its content delivery network. To serve the file, Hugging Face sees your IP address, the file requested, and that the request came from "EdgeDraft-Android", and handles this under its own privacy policy. The app checks each file against a fixed checksum and rejects it if it does not match. Nothing from your recordings or meetings is part of these requests.

Google Play. Installing and updating the app, and any purchase of Pro, go through Google Play and are covered by Google's privacy policy. If we add server-side purchase checks in future, we will update this policy before doing so.

Files you export, share or copy. Export saves a PDF, text or Markdown file to your phone's Downloads/EdgeDraft folder. These files are not encrypted and sit outside the app's protection. Share hands the content to the app you pick. Copy puts it on the clipboard, which other apps on your phone may be able to read. We do not receive any of this.

If you contact us. Your email address and what you write reach us, and we use them only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We keep them as long as we need for that, and delete them on request unless the law requires us to keep them.

What we do not do. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software. We do not sell or share your personal information. The part of the app that draws its screens is technically blocked from contacting any server.

6. How your data is protected

  • Saved meetings, the People Directory and any unsaved transcript are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is held in the Android Keystore and does not leave your phone.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for the app, so your meetings are not copied to Google's backup. The result is that they cannot be restored onto a new phone, and if the Keystore key is lost the old data cannot be read.
  • The release version of the app does not write the words of your transcripts to the Android system log.
  • Limits. There is no app lock or biometric prompt, so anyone who can unlock your phone can read your meetings. Exported files are not encrypted. If you used an earlier version that stored meetings unencrypted, remnants can stay in Android's web-view storage until Android tidies it; clearing the app's data removes them. No system is perfectly secure.

7. How long we keep things, and deleting them

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you remove it.

  • A meeting: delete it in the Summaries tab.
  • Models: delete them in Setup.
  • Everything: clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Exported files: delete them yourself from Downloads/EdgeDraft. Uninstalling the app does not remove them.
  • Audio: not kept, so there is nothing to delete.

Because we hold none of your content, we cannot retrieve or restore it for you.

8. Recording other people

You decide when to record and you are responsible for telling people and getting their permission, as the law where you are requires. The app reminds you before your first recording. We never receive recordings, transcripts or summaries, so we cannot find, correct or delete them. If you were recorded and want something removed, ask the person who recorded you.

For the content you create, you are in control of it, and if you use the app for work or to record others you may be responsible under data protection law as the "controller". We are the controller only of the small amount of information we hold ourselves, which is your correspondence with us.
